About Tornadoes

More than just a team — we are a family.

What began in 1989 as a small swim club with just 10 swimmers has grown into Jamaica’s premier aquatic program, encompassing competitive swimming, Learn to Swim, artistic swimming, water polo, triathlon, open water swimming, and masters swimming.

Founded by Coach Jacky Walter, Kim Ingleton, and Pat Compust, Tornadoes Swim Club is built on the core values of perseverance, teamwork, and excellence, with family at its heart.

Today, Tornadoes has over 250 athletes competing at local, regional, and international levels.
