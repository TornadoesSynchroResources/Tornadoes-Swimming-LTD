Learn to Swim

More than swimming lessons.

At Tornadoes, we believe learning to swim is about much more than learning strokes.

Our programme helps swimmers develop water confidence, safety awareness, technique and independence in a positive and supportive environment.

Our Programme

A pathway for every swimmer.

From first experiences in the water to confident independent swimming, our programme provides a clear progression.

01

Water Confidence

Helping swimmers become comfortable, relaxed and confident in the aquatic environment.

02

Swimming Skills

Developing fundamental skills including floating, breathing, kicking and movement through the water.

03

Stroke Development

Building proper technique and introducing the four competitive swimming strokes.

04

Water Safety

Developing essential skills that help swimmers become safer and more capable around water.

05

Confidence

Creating positive experiences that encourage swimmers to enjoy learning and challenge themselves.

06

Progression

Preparing swimmers who are ready to continue into more advanced swimming programmes.

Swimming Levels

Find your swimmer's starting point.

Our instructors assess each swimmer and help them progress at an appropriate pace.

Level 1

Introduction to the water, confidence and basic movement.

Level 2

Developing independent swimming and fundamental skills.

Level 3

Building stronger technique, endurance and stroke skills.

Level 4+

Advanced skills and preparation for continued swimming.
